# Auralis 0.3 — test checklist
1. Open in landscape and confirm forest, buildings and NPCs are visible.
2. Walk in all eight directions; check camera and collision.
3. Move east/southeast from the crossroads to find wolves.
4. Approach a wolf and tap attack within 100 world units (automatic nearest-target selection); damage number should appear.
5. Defeat a wolf: it disappears, XP increases, and it respawns after ~11 seconds.
6. Let a wolf approach: HP should decrease. At zero HP hero respawns in the village (1030,1140) with 40% HP/MP.
7. Reload the page: hero coordinates, HP, level and XP should persist.
8. Confirm HP (red), MP (blue), XP (gold) bars are visible and XP fills after kills.\n9. Note: combat graphics, balance, death flow, loot, NPC interactions are still prototypes.
